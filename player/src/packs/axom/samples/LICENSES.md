# Licenses — Axom (Bihu) pack samples

Only two provenances are allowed in this directory:

1. **Self-recorded** (preferred): the contributor records the instrument
   themselves and releases the file under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
   (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Note the file,
   contributor, and date below.
2. **CC0 / public-domain** third-party sources: note the exact source URL,
   the publication date, and the CC0 dedication or public-domain reason.

Anything else — YouTube rips, commercial sample libraries, field
recordings of unclear provenance — will be removed.

## Template (copy per file)

- **File:** `dotora-C5.mp3`
- **Provenance:** self-recorded by <name> on <date>
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal
